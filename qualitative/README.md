Prompt:

> Create a kitchen with a central table, two chairs, a refrigerator, and a
> cabinet. Both chairs and the refrigerator should face the table.

The before condition places the red chair intersecting the table and points the refrigerator away from the table. The after condition separates and orients the chairs and points the refrigerator toward the table.
